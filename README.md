# 🚀 ProductIQ — AI-Powered Product Intelligence Platform

> Transform fragmented industrial product information into structured, validated, searchable, and commerce-ready product intelligence.

ProductIQ is an AI-powered product intelligence platform designed to automate the extraction, enrichment, validation, and organization of industrial product information from technical datasheets, PDFs, scanned documents, images, CSV files, and limited product descriptions.

It combines **multimodal AI extraction, RAG grounding, confidence auditing, Knowledge Graph validation, and Human-in-the-Loop review** into a single workflow.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Industrial manufacturers and suppliers maintain large amounts of product information across:

- Technical datasheets
- PDFs
- Scanned documents
- Product catalogs
- Images
- CSV files
- Short product descriptions

This information is often unstructured, inconsistent, incomplete, and difficult to validate.

Manual catalog preparation requires significant time and can introduce:

- Missing product attributes
- Incorrect specifications
- Inconsistent terminology
- Duplicate records
- Physical/specification inconsistencies
- Low confidence in AI-generated information

ProductIQ addresses this problem by turning fragmented product information into structured and validated product intelligence.
